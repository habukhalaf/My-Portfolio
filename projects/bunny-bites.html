Bunny Bites

A complete brand identity for Bunny Bites, a local online cookie business known for its half chocolate chip, half brownie cookie. I designed everything from the logo and menu to packaging, photography, and social content.

01 / 08

Logo

The primary logo, built around the brand's signature half chocolate chip, half brownie cookie. It puts the product right at the heart of the identity.

02 / 08

Typography & colour palette

The Bunny Bites wordmark and a six-colour palette drawn from rich chocolate tones. Together they set the decadent feel that runs through every touchpoint.

03 / 08

Bunny mascot

A bunny mascot holding a cookie, shown on brown and slate backgrounds. It gives the brand a friendly character for packaging stickers and social posts.

04 / 08

Cookie illustration

Illustrations of the signature half chocolate chip, half brownie cookie. They let the product appear consistently across print and social, even without a photo.

05 / 08

Business cards

Front and back business cards for the brand. They carry the logo and palette into a take-home piece for customers.

06 / 08

Menu

A three-page menu covering the cookie types, sizes and prices, and sharing offers. I kept the layout simple so customers could order at a glance.

07 / 08

Social media posts

Posts from the brand's Instagram grid, designed as a cohesive set. They showcase the cookies while keeping the feed recognisably on-brand.

08 / 08

Instagram reel

Product styling, filming, and editing for the brand's Instagram reels.
