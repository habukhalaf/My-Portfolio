CSCCTFv7 2026

A cinematic, movie theatre-themed identity for CSCCTFv7, PSUT's annual 24-hour cybersecurity competition. As Media Lead, I handled the branding, video, print, and photography, and debuted the club's pixel mascot, Sushi.

Announcement video

Colour & type

The “Final Cut: Breach” title treatment and the cinema-inspired palette behind the whole event. Together they set the movie theatre tone for every poster, badge, and video that followed.

03 / 10

Logo

The CSCCTFv7 shield logo, filled with popcorn to bring the club's mark into the cinema theme. It also doubled as one of the event stickers.

04 / 10

Badges

Three badge variants for competitors, volunteers, and challenge writers, each with a front and back. Distinct designs made every role easy to spot across the 24-hour event.

05 / 10

Badge & stickers in hand

The printed badge and stickers together on a lanyard at the event. It shows how the physical pieces came together as one cohesive set.

06 / 10

Sticker set

Cinema-themed stickers, from a clapperboard and movie ticket to Sushi in a popcorn box. This set marked the official debut of Sushi, the club's pixel art mascot created in Aseprite.

07 / 10

Schedule

The event schedule, designed as a row of VHS tape spines. Turning a practical timetable into a shelf of tapes kept even the logistics firmly on theme.

08 / 10

Sponsor posts

Sponsor announcements for INE and PwnedLabs styled as popcorn boxes, alongside a cinema-style sponsors poster. Each partner stayed front and centre without breaking the movie world.

09 / 10

Video assets

Illustrated backgrounds of a cinema lobby and concession stand, created for the animated announcement video. They set the scene for the story the video tells.

10 / 10

Winners' cheque

The oversized first-place cheque, designed as a cinema ticket and shown here with the winning team. It closed the event with a piece made for the photos.
