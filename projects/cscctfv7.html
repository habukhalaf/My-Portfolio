CSCCTFv7 2026

Overview:
CSCCTFv7 is an annual 24 hour on site cybersecurity competition for students at Princess Sumaya University for Technology. As Media Lead, I developed a complete visual identity built around a cinematic, movie theater theme and officially introduced the club's new pixel art mascot, Sushi, created in Aseprite.

I directed, filmed, and edited a promotional Instagram teaser, alongside illustrating custom assets for a separate animated announcement video. For the live event, I managed social media, delivered web graphics, and designed physical assets including posters, three badge variants, stickers, and the oversized winners' cheque while also serving as the official event photographer.
