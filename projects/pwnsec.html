PWNSEC CTF 2026

Overview:
For PWNSEC CTF 2026, I designed a series of promotional posters centered around the event's "Human vs. Cyborg" theme. Working closely with their established logo style and provided storyline, I illustrated custom pixel art characters, a human and a cyborg, that served as the focal point for the campaign.

These character illustrations were featured across the main CTF announcement poster and two supplementary promotional graphics, ensuring a cohesive, story driven visual experience that perfectly matched the competition's aesthetic.
