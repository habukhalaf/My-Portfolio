PWNSEC CTF 2026

A pixel art poster campaign for PWNSEC CTF 2026, built around the event's “Humans vs Cyborgs” storyline. I illustrated the characters and designed the announcement and story posters within the event's existing brand.

Deliverables:
  • → Event Poster Design
  • → Pixel Art & Character Illustration
  • → Visual Storytelling
  • → Brand Alignment
01 / 07

Main announcement poster

The centrepiece of the campaign, setting the human and the cyborg face to face beneath the event's pixel logo. I built the composition around the “Choose your side” call to action so the storyline reads at a glance.

02 / 07

Logo & title

The event's established pixel logo and “Humans vs Cyborgs” title, which I worked within rather than redesigning. Matching their chunky, retro style kept every new illustration aligned with the existing brand.

03 / 07

Story posters

Two supplementary graphics that give each side of the story its own spotlight. Released as a pair, they carried the narrative beyond the main poster and across the campaign.

04 / 07

Character sprites

Full-body pixel art of the human and the cyborg, illustrated from the event's storyline. Their mirrored running poses were designed to clash head-on across the main poster.

05 / 07

Character portraits

Close-up pixel portraits of both characters, giving each one a distinct personality. The headshots let the characters work at smaller sizes across the supporting graphics.

06 / 07

Certificates

Certificates of achievement for the top players on the cyborg and human leaderboards. Each carries its side's identity, extending the story right to the end of the competition.

07 / 07

Flag

A pixel art flag drawn to match the banners in the event logo. Small assets like this kept the retro, game-inspired world consistent across every piece.
