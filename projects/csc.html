Cyber security club PSUT 2025-2026

Overview:
As Media Lead for the Cyber Security Club during the 2025–2026 academic year, I completely reimagined the organization's visual identity, breaking away from the cliché green and black hacker aesthetic. I kicked off my term by designing a highly customized logo infused with typography that highlighted the current committee and the club's decade long history.

Throughout the year, I flexibly adapted our branding to fit various event themes, creating dynamic graphics for national online CTFs, beginner workshops, and the MetaCTF 2025 announcement. Beyond graphic design and web assets, I heavily expanded the club's media presence through video production filming and editing high energy recap videos that beautifully captured our live events, club trips, and the ongoing journeys of our members.
