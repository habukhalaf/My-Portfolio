Cyber security club PSUT 2025-2026

A year-long rebrand of the Cyber Security Club at PSUT for the 2025-2026 academic year. As Media Lead, I redesigned the club's identity and produced its posters, announcements, merch, and recap videos.

01 / 10

Committee name logo

The club logo rebuilt from the names of this year's committee members, in green and pink editions. It celebrates the people behind the club and its decade-long history.

02 / 10

Shirt designs

Club shirts featuring a pixel dinosaur and a member@cscpsut terminal prompt, in a green edition and a pink edition with a bow. They nod to the club's roots without falling back on the usual hacker clichés.

03 / 10

Shirts in the wild

Members wearing the finished shirts around campus. Seeing the designs worn day to day was the real test of the new identity.

04 / 10

Club recap

Capturing the club's live events, trips, and the ongoing journeys of our members throughout the year.

05 / 10

Pixel fox

A bow-tie variation of Sushi, the club's pixel art fox mascot. Character pieces like this gave the refreshed brand a friendly face across the club's content.

06 / 10

CPTC recap

A recap video I filmed and edited as Media Lead to announce and celebrate the club's winning CPTC team. It turned the team's result into shareable content for the club's audience.

07 / 10

Workshop posters

Posters for the beginner workshops and the cryptography, blue team, and web exploitation trainings. Each series takes its own visual idea, from street signs to stacked TVs, while staying recognisably part of the club.

08 / 10

Event announcements

Announcements for MetaCTF 2025, styled as a boarding pass, and the Ramadan CTF. I adapted the club's branding to fit each event's theme.

09 / 10

Committee recruitment

A “Join Our Committee” post recruiting media officers. It helped grow the team behind the club's expanding media presence.

10 / 10

Themed posts

The club logo in a military camouflage treatment, paired with a national-themed post featuring the Jordanian flag and a falcon. They show how flexibly the identity adapts to different occasions.
