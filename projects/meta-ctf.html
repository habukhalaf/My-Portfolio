META CTF 2026

Overview:
MetaCTF 2026 is a nationwide cybersecurity competition for students and industry professionals in Jordan. As Media Lead for the Cyber Security Club, I led the visual identity across the two round event, anchoring the brand in a high adrenaline Formula 1 theme.

I adapted the original logo, created a custom title font, and produced a high energy promo video that drove strong social media engagement for the qualifiers. My work spanned both digital and physical touchpoints from web platform assets and marketing posters to custom event badges and a story driven sticker set featuring the club's pixel art mascot, Sushi.

* The MetaCTF 2026 finals are coming soon in October 2026!
