META CTF 2026

A Formula 1-themed identity for MetaCTF 2026, a nationwide cybersecurity competition in Jordan. As Media Lead for the Cyber Security Club, I led the visuals from the promo video to badges, stickers, and venue print.

* The MetaCTF 2026 finals are coming soon in October 2026!

01 / 12

Promo video

A high-energy Formula 1-themed promo that drove strong social media engagement for the qualifiers.

02 / 12

F1 kinetic teaser

A motion graphics teaser I animated in After Effects, bringing the Formula 1 theme to life in a fast, kinetic edit. It carries the campaign's racing energy from print into motion.

03 / 12

Title type

The custom title font I created for META CTF 26, built to feel as fast and bold as Formula 1 livery. It anchors the whole campaign, from posters to badges.

04 / 12

Colour palette

A focused three-colour palette running from cream to deep racing red. Keeping it tight gave every digital and printed asset one consistent, high-adrenaline look.

05 / 12

Qualifier announcements

Announcement posters for the qualifying round, including a dedicated call for industry professionals. The racetrack setting introduced the F1 theme from the very first post.

06 / 12

Sponsor posts

Sponsor features styled as stamps, recognising the Central Bank of Jordan as platinum sponsor and OffSec as prize sponsor. The format let each partner stand out while staying on-brand.

07 / 12

Web platform assets

Banners for the qualifier and finals registration forms, plus the competition platform background. They carried the identity into the spaces competitors spent the most time in.

08 / 12

Backdrop & banner

Large-format print for the venue: a “Welcome to MetaCTF” stage backdrop and a sponsor banner. Both were designed to hold up at scale and in event photos.

09 / 12

Badges

A full badge system for competitors, VIP guests, media, and volunteers, plus team principal badges for each challenge category. Each category borrows from a different F1 team, so every role is recognisable at a glance.

10 / 12

Team shirts

Shirt designs for competitors, media, volunteers, and challenge writers. Each role gets its own look within the same racing-inspired system.

11 / 12

Sticker set

A story-driven sticker set starring Sushi, the club's pixel art mascot, suited up and behind the wheel. Mixing the mascot with racing icons made the swag feel collectable.

12 / 12

Video prop

A terminal screen prop created for the promo video. It ties the hacking side of the competition into the racing theme on screen.
